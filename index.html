It is easy to control the gain and offset of a circuit by the analog potentiometer and by hand; yet, when you want a circuit to control the gain and offset of its input by itself, it would not be easy as before. In this project, I made a circuit and wrote a program on ARDUINO to control the gain and offset of inputs without human contribution. At the end of the project, we had a circuit which took twelve brain signals as its input and amplified them and then controlled the gain and offset of these signals and revealed them in the best state.

Research Assistant
Supervisor: DR. Reshad Hosseini

Angular Gaussian method is a new robust approach for subspace recovery; however, this method was not used for multiple subspaces. In this project, I implemented Angular Gaussian method and some other state-of-the-art methods such as SSC and LRR which was robust and used for multiple subspaces. I used these methods for recovering subspaces in some artificial and real data. In two and three dimensions I plotted subspaces and calculated classification error and also, as the second error, I calculated the angle between real subspaces and the subspaces which were given as the output of the methods. For higher dimensions, I just calculated classification error. After that, I attempted to compare these approaches based on their errors on the same data.I used MATLAB for this project.
